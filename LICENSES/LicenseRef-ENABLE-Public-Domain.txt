The ENABLE word list (Enhanced North American Benchmark LExicon), compiled by Alan Beale with contributions from
M. Cooper.

Its own README states: "The ENABLE master word list, WORD.LST, is herewith formally released into the Public Domain.
Anyone is free to use it or distribute it in any manner they see fit." It asks that the source be mentioned and its
compilers credited as the originators of the list, and that nobody copyright or restrict redistribution of the list
itself.

The file src/EQLWikiEditorAssistant.Core/Glyphs/enable-i-l-words.txt is a subset of that list, taken from enable1.txt
(172,823 words) as distributed at https://github.com/dolph/dictionary. It remains in the public domain: the MIT licence
that covers the rest of this repository does not apply to it, and nothing here restricts its redistribution.
