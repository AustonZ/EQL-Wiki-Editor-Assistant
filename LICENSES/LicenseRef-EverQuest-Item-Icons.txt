The files under game_assets/item_icons/ are item icon artwork extracted from the
EverQuest Legends game client. They belong to the game's publisher,
Daybreak Game Company LLC, or its licensors.

They are NOT covered by this project's MIT licence, and this project grants
no licence to them of any kind. Copying them out of this repository does not
make them free to use.

Why they are here: the EQL Wiki Editor Assistant identifies an item's icon by
comparing the in-game artwork against these files, and can upload the matching
one to the EverQuest Legends Wiki. For many years the publisher has allowed
EverQuest item icons to appear on fan wikis and item databases, which is the
use made of them here. That is the publisher's tolerance, not a licence, and
it covers nothing beyond that use.

Anyone wanting to use these files for any other purpose (another project, a
dataset, training a model, redistribution) needs the publisher's permission.

If the rights holder asks for them to be removed, they will be.
