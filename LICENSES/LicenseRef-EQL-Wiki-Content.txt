The page files under tests/EQLWikiEditorAssistant.Tests/Wiki/Fixtures/ are
copies of item pages from the EverQuest Legends Wiki (https://eqlwiki.com),
written by that wiki's contributors. docs/media/demo.gif shows part of one
such page as the wiki renders it. Their copyright belongs to those contributors.

They are NOT covered by this project's MIT license. The wiki itself names no
license for its content: when checked on 2026-10-08, its edit notice said
contributions are "released under the" license it then leaves blank.

They are kept as test fixtures because real pages, with all their
inconsistencies, are what the Assistant has to read and edit correctly, and
the recording shows what the Assistant does to one. They are used for nothing
else.
